Challenge: The AI Pair Engineer (#2)
Project: CodeQuorum: https://github.com/suboss87/CodeQuorum

SUMMARY (100 words):

Most code review tools simulate one reviewer. Real review is a panel: a pragmatist
who wants to ship, a purist who demands correctness, an operator who's been paged at
3am. They disagree. That disagreement is the signal.

CodeQuorum orchestrates three specialist agents in parallel using LangGraph, then routes
all findings to a synthesis agent that identifies consensus (fix it, quorum reached)
versus conflict (your call, genuine tradeoff). Confidence per finding comes from
inter-agent agreement, not from one LLM self-assessing certainty. This is the exact
multi-agent orchestration pattern needed for enterprise WorkOS: specialist agents,
parallel execution, structured handoff, disagreement as a first-class output.
